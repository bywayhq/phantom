deadline and collection-channel failures retain actual `Elapsed` and `RecvError`
through their contextual error source. Literal existing messages remain.

an actual `get_io()` disconnect recognized by the existing narrow classifier:
BrokenPipe, ConnectionReset, ConnectionAborted, or UnexpectedEof. PermissionDenied,
protocol, and TLS failures propagate. Resolved backend `proto/connection.rs`
613–637 explains that a server with no buffered output can itself turn EOF into
clean closure; 280–298 retains unrelated GOAWAY errors. `server.rs` 499–533
documents driver progress, and `error.rs` 77–97 restricts `get_io()` to I/O errors.
The baseline's real healthy-close positive and explicitly injected read-fault
negative remain unchanged. This is a fixture remedy, not a production H2 defect.
