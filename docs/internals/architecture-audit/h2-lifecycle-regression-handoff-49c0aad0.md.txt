## Preserved ordinary behavior

Against approved e0, 18 original methods have identical operation tokens.
Six original methods change their caller expression; they are not claimed
byte-identical. Their profiles, routes, capture literals, ordered fields,
ALPN, bodies, disconnect conditions and timers stay unchanged:

- `h2_origin_over_h2_proxy_tunnel_completes_request`: the actual inline origin
  closure calls concrete `serve_h2_origin(..., None)`. Removing the transparent
  disabled reader adapter and remapping its two parameter names reconstructs
  the original closure's complete operation tokens exactly.
- `h2_forwarding_fails_after_a_second_challenge`: direct peer await becomes
  `finish_forward_exchange(Ok(()), peer)` before the same request-count assertion.
- `h2_connect_sends_the_captured_profile_fields`,
  `h2_connect_closes_the_challenged_stream_as_the_profile_does`, and
  `h2_wss_connect_sends_the_captured_profile_fields`: the formerly discarded
  request result passes through `observe_rejection`, which still discards it.
- `websocket_tunnels_use_the_connection_the_profile_gives_them`: direct echo
  await becomes concrete `finish_websocket_exchange(Ok(()), peer)`.

The complete decoder child and all adopted capture/HPACK/deadline helper tokens
match e0. Body reconstruction and token comparison are mechanical checks that
support the manual review; they do not independently grant semantic coverage.

The actual relay closures are extracted to concrete same-owner functions.
Their ignored error branches return `Ok(())` instead of unit solely to expose
outcomes; no-fault operation ordering remains the same. Origin/listener unit
workers similarly gain a Result seam, retaining their existing swallowed
errors. Raw JoinHandles are retained in explicit fixture scaffolds while old
callers detach them through equivalent adapters. This is baseline scaffolding,
not a corrected ownership contract.
