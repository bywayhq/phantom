## Limits

- ConnectionPeer Drop requests cancellation; it cannot await a join. Cancellation, panic, outer timeout or runtime loss is not claimed to synchronously reap a task.
- Explicit returned-error cleanup uses the existing bounded ConnectionPeer stop and route outcome collector. The outer integration deadline can cancel this cleanup, leaving Drop cancellation requests rather than a completed join.
- Destruction controls witness actual fixture object destruction with external Client/runtime alive before later backup abort; a notification is not itself a JoinHandle wait result.
- Completed failure readiness controls use current-thread Tokio tests. Their destruction notifications do not imply a general scheduling or operating-system teardown guarantee.
- Target literal IPv6 control observes actual ATYP4 decoding over IPv4 loopback forwarding; it does not prove an IPv6 transport or browser acceptance.
- Full gates, production route parity and full codebase completion remain open.
